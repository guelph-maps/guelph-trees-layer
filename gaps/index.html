Copying species or girth from this overlay into OSM is in effect an attribute import, and importing individual trees is contested in OSM — add what you can check yourself (a crown on imagery, a species on the ground); nothing here is meant to be bulk-copied. OSM circumference is in metres (DBH × π / 100; the City's DBH is in cm). OSM trees with no City tree are not listed: woodlots, back yards and campus trees the City does not inventory.
